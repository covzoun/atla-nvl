# Glossary.md

## Purpose
This glossary serves as the project's canonical vocabulary reference. It defines recurring project-specific terms, identities, techniques, objects, organizations, and naming conventions so future writers and AI models can maintain consistent terminology without searching across multiple canon documents.

This file is intentionally concise. It summarizes established concepts rather than replacing the detailed explanations found in the other project files. Where a conflict exists, the source document referenced by an entry should be treated as authoritative.

---

# Characters

## Jake Namiq
**Status:** Locked
Southern Water Tribe-born waterbender raised in the Fire Nation by Haru and Emi. Jake serves as the project's primary protagonist and emotional POV anchor. His relationship with Zuko forms the central emotional axis of the trilogy.

## Zuko
**Status:** Locked
Fire Nation prince exiled following the Agni Kai. Zuko functions as the project's primary secondary protagonist and the other half of its central relationship. His personal growth remains rooted in ATLA canon while being recontextualized through Jake's presence.

## Iroh
**Status:** Locked
Former Fire Nation general and Zuko's uncle. Within this project, Iroh becomes Jake's principal mentor during exile, the political architect of Jake's survival after the Agni Kai, and the emotional foundation of the exile household.

## Haru
**Status:** Locked
One of Jake's adoptive parents and a palace servant attached to the royal household. Haru helps raise Jake inside the Fire Nation while concealing his true origins.

## Emi
**Status:** Locked
One of Jake's adoptive parents and a palace servant attached to the royal household. Emi shares responsibility for protecting Jake's identity and raising him within palace life.

## Ozai
**Status:** Locked
Fire Lord of the Fire Nation. Ozai's judgment during the Agni Kai exposes Jake's hidden identity and results in both boys' exile.

## Azula
**Status:** Locked
Fire Nation princess. Although she does not fully understand Jake's identity during the Agni Kai itself, she gradually reconstructs the truth through the political aftermath.

## Team Avatar
**Status:** Locked
Collective term for Aang and his companions. Team Avatar remains central to ATLA's original story while gradually reinterpreting Jake and Zuko through their encounters across the trilogy.

# Aliases and Identities

## White Heron
**Status:** Locked
Jake's anonymous field identity, first used during Book 1 during the events of *The Blue Spirit*. White Heron allows Jake to operate without revealing his identity, his connection to Zuko, or his status as a waterbender. The identity emphasizes protection, guidance, rescue, and quiet resilience rather than direct assault.

## Blue Spirit
**Status:** Locked
Zuko's established covert identity used during dangerous operations requiring anonymity. Within this project, Blue Spirit remains Zuko's identity while White Heron develops as its narrative counterpart rather than its replacement.

# Techniques & Combat

## Flowing Guard
**Status:** Locked
Jake's signature defensive waterbending style, prioritizing redirection and protection over overwhelming force. *(Full mechanics and philosophy are centralized in `Bending_Styles.md`).*

## Steam Technique
**Status:** Locked
Jake's dangerous emergency waterbending technique developed during Book 3. It rapidly transfers heat between Jake's body and water to generate violent bursts of steam at significant physical cost. *(Full mechanics are centralized in `Bending_Styles.md`).*

## White Heron Combat Philosophy
**Status:** Locked
The non-bending expression of Flowing Guard, prioritizing protection and redirection through physical movement. *(Full mechanics are centralized in `Bending_Styles.md`).*

# Objects

## White Heron Mask
**Status:** Locked
The mask worn while Jake operates as White Heron. Beyond concealing his identity, it connects him to an existing Fire Nation folktale whose meaning gradually evolves through his actions.

## White Heron Weapon
**Status:** Locked
Jake's signature unnamed flexible cord weapon used while acting as White Heron. *(Full physical description and combat applications are centralized in `Bending_Styles.md`).*

## Jake's Gloves
**Status:** Locked
Gloves given to Jake by Iroh after the Agni Kai to protect his burned hands and conceal the scars. They become a recurring visual reminder of recovery, concealment, and unresolved trauma.

## Jake's Keepsake
**Status:** Locked
A simple handmade braided cord charm created by Haru. Jake carries it from his former home as a personal reminder of the family that raised him. It has no special abilities or connection to bending; it is a private anchor to his past.

## Appa
**Status:** Locked
Aang's sky bison and primary mode of transportation. Appa is massive — six-legged, covered in thick white fur, with a broad flat skull and small dark eyes. Sky bisons do not have wings; they fly by levitating using airbending. Appa can carry multiple passengers on his back. He is one of the last sky bisons, having survived the extinction of the Air Nomads. His fur is white, not brown. His eyes are small and dark. His horns curve backward from his skull.

# Locations

## Southern Water Tribe
**Status:** Locked
Jake's birthplace. Although central to his biological origins, it is not the home he consciously remembers at the beginning of the story.

## Fire Nation Royal Palace
**Status:** Locked
Jake's childhood home and workplace before exile. It is where he is raised by Haru and Emi, befriends Zuko, and where the Agni Kai permanently changes his life.

## Exile Ship
**Status:** Locked
The vessel shared by Zuko, Iroh, Jake, and the exile crew throughout the three-year exile preceding Book 1. It functions as the primary setting of Jake and Zuko's shared early adulthood.

## Agni Kai
**Status:** Locked
The defining turning point of the project. Jake's intervention during Ozai's punishment of Zuko exposes his hidden waterbending, scars his hands, destroys both boys' lives within the Fire Nation, and begins their shared exile.

# Organizations and Groups

## Royal Household
**Status:** Locked
The palace community within which Haru, Emi, Jake, and the royal family coexist prior to exile. Jake's place within it is practical and lived rather than formally legitimate.

## Palace Servants
**Status:** Locked
The servants attached to the royal household. Jake grows up among them and eventually works beside Haru and Emi before the Agni Kai.

## Exile Crew
**Status:** Locked
The Fire Nation sailors and officers serving aboard Zuko's ship. By Book 1, they know Jake is a waterbender and regard him as a permanent member of the exile rather than a temporary passenger.

# Symbols and Motifs

## White Heron
A recurring symbol of guidance, mercy, quiet protection, resilience, and hope during conflict. Jake inherits the existing folklore and gradually reshapes its meaning through his own actions.

## Blue Spirit
A recurring symbol of fractured honor, desperation, direct action, and survival through anonymity.

## Burn Scars
Jake's burned hands symbolize sacrifice, survival, hidden pain, and the lasting consequences of choosing to protect someone else.

## Gloves
More than protective clothing, Jake's gloves symbolize concealment, healing, emotional restraint, and the visible cost of the Agni Kai.

## Home and Belonging
One of the project's central themes. Jake's journey continually explores the tension between birthplace, upbringing, chosen family, and the meaning of home.

## Protection Over Victory
The guiding philosophy shared across Jake's character arc, Flowing Guard, and White Heron. Success is measured first by preserving life rather than defeating opponents.

# Naming Conventions

## Strict Capitalization
To prevent inconsistent capitalization or accidental renaming, the following terms are fixed and must always be capitalized:

**Nations, Cultures, and Groups**
- Fire Nation
- Earth Kingdom
- Water Tribe
- Southern Water Tribe
- Northern Water Tribe
- Air Nomads
- Fire Nation Royal Family

**Bending and Combat Terms**
- Waterbending
- Firebending
- Earthbending
- Airbending
- Bending
- Flowing Guard
- White Heron
- Blue Spirit

**Titles and Important Roles**
- Avatar
- Fire Lord
- Crown Prince
- Prince Zuko
- General Iroh
- Commander Zhao

**Locations**
- Ba Sing Se
- Agni Kai
- Southern Air Temple
- Northern Water Tribe
- Fire Nation Capital

**Project-Specific Terms**
- Jake Namiq
- Zuko
- White Heron
- Blue Spirit
- Flowing Guard

*(Note: "Jake's keepsake" should remain lowercase in prose to reflect its status as an ordinary, practical object rather than a named artifact).*

## Character Names
After introduction, refer to the protagonist simply as **Jake** in narrative prose.
Reserve **Jake Namiq** for documentation, formal identification, first introduction, or official dialogue where appropriate.

## White Heron Terminology
**Preferred:** White Heron, the White Heron, anonymous identity, covert identity, field identity.
**Avoid:** superhero, vigilante, White Bird, White Crane, Heron Spirit.

## Blue Spirit Terminology
Treat Blue Spirit as Zuko's established covert identity rather than merely describing it as a mask.

## Flowing Guard
Always use the complete name **Flowing Guard**. Do not shorten or rename the style.

## White Heron Weapon
The weapon is intentionally unnamed. In prose, refer to it using functional descriptions such as: *rope dart, weighted cord, flexible cord weapon, braided cord weapon,* or *flexible weapon*. Do not invent a permanent name or treat it as a legendary artifact.

## Jake's Upbringing
**Prefer:** raised in the Fire Nation, raised by Haru and Emi, Fire Nation upbringing.
**Avoid:** wording that implies Jake is ethnically Fire Nation by birth.

## Relationship Terminology
For Book 1, describe Jake and Zuko as: closest companions, closest friends, deeply bonded, emotionally interdependent. Avoid romantic labels before the relationship has canonically reached that stage.
