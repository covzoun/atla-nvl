# Chapter Generation

The approved Chapter Blueprint Package (titled "Chapter_[Number]_Blueprint.md") has been attached.

It contains:

• Developmental Chapter Blueprint
• Canon Anchor
• Cleaned Canon Reference

Generate Scene 1 only.

--------------------------------------------------
YOUR TASK
--------------------------------------------------

Transform the approved blueprint into immersive novel prose.

The Developmental Chapter Blueprint determines:

• scene purpose
• structure
• pacing
• emotional progression
• relationship progression

The Canon Anchor determines:

• preserved canon events
• important sequence details
• recognizable actions
• cause and effect

The Cleaned Canon Reference provides:

• source details
• original context
• dialogue intent
• environmental information

Follow all three.

--------------------------------------------------
OPENING CONTINUITY CHECK
--------------------------------------------------

Before writing:

Compare:

• previous chapter ending
• current scene opening

Ensure:

• location matches
• timeline matches
• character positions match
• equipment matches
• emotional states continue naturally

Do not restart completed actions.

Do not recreate previous scenes.

--------------------------------------------------
POV REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Write through the assigned POV character.

Only include information the POV character could reasonably know.

Do not:

• reveal unknown names
• reveal private thoughts
• reveal unseen events
• reveal information from audience knowledge

When encountering unknown characters:

Describe them through observation until their identity becomes known naturally.

--------------------------------------------------
CANON REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Do not reduce canon scenes into broad summaries.

Preserve:

• event order
• important actions
• character interactions
• physical staging
• consequences

Expand through:

• atmosphere
• sensory detail
• emotional observation
• dialogue
• physical movement

Do not replace canon actions with similar but different events.

--------------------------------------------------
PROSE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Prioritize:

• clarity
• immersion
• emotional realism
• readable sentence structure

The reader should always understand:

• who is acting
• who is speaking
• what happened
• where the characters are

--------------------------------------------------
PRE-WRITING CHECK
--------------------------------------------------

Before writing, silently confirm:

• You know only what the POV character knows. No future significance, no hidden motives, no audience knowledge.

• Nothing repeats or resets earlier chapters (established information, emotional states, preparations).

• Jake has active choices in this scene; his waterbending stays primary over White Heron methods.

• Emotional and relationship states match State_Tracker.md baselines; progression is gradual, not leapt to.

• Canon beats from the blueprint stay load-bearing; original material supports them rather than overshadowing them.

• Style_Guide.md banned patterns (therapy-speak, stock gestures, explained emotions, poetic vagueness, stated themes) are absent.

--------------------------------------------------
SKILLS (only if present in your environment — otherwise ignore silently and proceed)
--------------------------------------------------

• The /humanizer skill (human-sounding prose) is PERMITTED — use it when available, in whichever mode your environment supports. If you can load its skill file as a tool/skill, you may run the finished scene through it as an explicit revision pass AFTER drafting; otherwise apply its principles inline while drafting (no AI tells, no stock phrasing). Either way it sits under the Style Guide in authority and never overrides the blueprint: a humanizer pass may change wording, rhythm, and micro-phrasing ONLY — never events, beats, dialogue intent, canon details, POV, or scene boundaries. If a humanizer suggestion conflicts with the blueprint, discard the suggestion.
• The /caveman skill (token-compressed speech) must NEVER touch the prose. The chapter text stays full-length, literary, and in the POV voice at all times — no compression, paraphrase, shortening, or caveman diction. The only non-prose output in this task is the fixed status tags below, which must stay character-exact. If that leaves caveman with nothing to do, leave it dormant.
