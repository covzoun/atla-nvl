# State_Tracker.md

## Purpose and Update Rules

This is the project's **single living continuity document**. It tracks both **story state** (injuries, inventory, location, objectives, timeline, unresolved threads) and **relationship state** (trust, tension, progression, active secrets). There is no separate relationship tracker.

- Updated by the Phase 3 Continuity Clerk after each completed chapter, then verified by the human at Gate 3.
- It must never rewrite locked canon or chronology (`Canon.md`, `Story_Bible.md`, `Relationships.md` remain authoritative). It records only changes explicitly supported by the completed chapter.
- **STATE sections** (Current Story Position, Jake's Current State, Zuko's Current State, Relationship States) are **replaced** to reflect the new current state. No history is appended here.
- **LOG sections** (Unresolved Threads, Character Introductions Log, Recent Events Summary) are **appended and pruned**: threads are added when opened and marked `Resolved` (not deleted) when closed; the Recent Events Summary holds only the last 1-2 chapters, dropping older entries as new ones are added.
- Unchanged sections are preserved verbatim.

## Recent Events Summary

Chapter 02: The ship breaches the ice wall and Zuko raids the village, beating back club and boomerang attacks. A penguin-riding arrow-marked boy reveals himself as the Avatar and surrenders, and is taken aboard with his staff secured in Zuko's quarters. The boy escapes via airbending, a mattress-slam, and a glider leap; Zuko drags both to the lower deck before a flying creature arrives with two riders. Zuko knocks the boy into the sea, triggering a white-eyed waterspout and deck-clearing wave that Jake subtly redirects to save crew; the unnamed girl freezes guards, a combined fire blast is deflected into the cliff, and the resulting avalanche buries the bow. Chapter 03: The ship docks at Ketu Harbor still scarred from the avalanche. Zhao has the crew interrogated, learns Zuko held then lost the Avatar, seizes the hunt with a search party departing within the hour, and goads Zuko over banishment, scar, and Jake's servant status until Zuko challenges him to a sunset Agni Kai. Zuko wins by striking the ground beside Zhao's head instead of flesh and walks away; Zhao fires at his back, Iroh intercepts and shames him, and the three return to the ship with Zuko vowed to capture the Avatar first.
