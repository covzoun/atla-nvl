# Core_Persona.md

# Purpose

This document defines Jake Namiq's stable psychological identity.
Unlike `Character_Guide.md`, which focuses on behavioral execution during scenes, this document establishes who Jake fundamentally is beneath circumstance.
Where a conflict exists regarding Jake's enduring personality, this file should be treated as authoritative unless overridden by `Canon.md`.

## Character Snapshot

* **Full name:** Jake Namiq
* **Age:** 13 at the time of the Agni Kai and exile; 16 at the start of Book 1
* **Role / identity summary:** Jake is a waterbender born in an unspecified Southern Water Tribe community and raised in the Fire Nation. His intervention during Zuko’s Agni Kai exposes his hidden identity and results in his exile alongside Zuko. Within the broader story, he functions as an active participant in canon events and as one half of the project’s central emotional axis with Zuko.
* **Cultural background / upbringing:** Jake was separated from his southern Water Tribe community at a very young age and raised in the Fire Nation by Haru and Emi, two palace servants. The community is not Wolf Cove, and its exact identity and fate remain unresolved. He grows up with little or no reliable memory of his original home and understands himself primarily as a Fire Nation orphan folded into palace labor and palace life. Before exile, the Fire Nation is the only home he consciously knows, and his sense of belonging is built around Haru and Emi, palace routines, and his long friendship with Zuko rather than around any understood Water Tribe identity.

## Physical Profile

* **Build / general appearance:** Jake is 5'5" and short beside Zuko, with a petite build that still reads as easy to underestimate at first glance. He carries light wiry strength from years of palace labor rather than formal combat training.
* **Features:** He has light-medium tan skin, muted blue eyes, dark softly wavy hair that tends to fall into his face, and a soft, youthful face that can make him seem younger than he is. His resting expression tends toward watchful, shy, or difficult to place until he relaxes.
* **Agni Kai scarring:** Jake’s most notable visual marker is the burn scarring on the undersides of both hands, running from the base of the palms through the fingertips, left by his intervention during Zuko’s Agni Kai.
* **Post-Agni-Kai visual markers:** After the Agni Kai, Jake regularly wears gloves given to him by Iroh, both to protect the damaged skin on his hands and to conceal the scarring. When anxious or stressed, he may pick or scratch at the scars beneath the gloves, sometimes hard enough to reopen the skin without immediately noticing.
* **Keepsake baseline (Locked):** Jake leaves the Fire Nation with one small practical keepsake: a simple handmade braided cord charm created by Haru. It is intimate and ordinary rather than visually prominent. It represents Haru's stability, Emi's care, and the home Jake lost. It has no special abilities or connection to bending; it functions purely as a private anchor to his past and a reminder of who he was before becoming the White Heron.

## Personality Profile

* **Public baseline:** Jake is quiet, introverted, and low-footprint by default. He does not naturally assume space is his to take, tends to listen before he speaks, and often comes across as though he is deciding whether he is allowed to join a conversation before actually doing so. His awkwardness reads less as social incompetence than as caution, self-consciousness, and a lifelong habit of making himself easy to accommodate.
* **Curiosity and attention:** Beneath the reserve, Jake is observant and curious. His curiosity tends to express itself in practical and interpersonal directions: he notices how systems work, what needs doing, how a room is functioning, and what people are feeling or avoiding. He pays attention to moods, routines, and shifts in behavior, often understanding more than he immediately says aloud.
* **Safe-person shift:** Jake is noticeably different with people he trusts. Around safe people, he becomes warmer, more talkative, and more visibly present. He asks more questions, lingers nearby, hovers to help, and lets more dry humor and quiet teasing show. Safety also brings out flashes of the younger self underneath his restraint: awkwardness, stubbornness, curiosity, and moments of quiet playfulness that suggest someone who had to grow up too early and does not often let that softer side surface in public.
* **Core loyalty pattern:** Jake is highly protective and tends to orient toward other people’s needs before his own. His first instinct under strain is often to help, steady, fix, or absorb rather than to step back and ask what he himself needs. He is emotionally attentive to others, but not emotionally transparent about himself.
* **Stress responses / defense mechanisms:** When frightened, ashamed, overwhelmed, or hurt, Jake gets practical first. He reaches for tasks, logistics, tending injuries, or immediate damage control so that he does not have to sit still inside what he feels. He also minimizes and edits himself by saying he is fine too quickly, omitting the worst parts, and offering fragments instead of full explanations. If pressed beyond that point, he tends to go quiet and stubborn rather than explosive, withdrawing, evading, or shutting down instead of openly unraveling. If someone he loves is threatened, however, his self-preservation drops sharply and he can become reckless in protective ways.
* **Self-worth and belonging pattern:** Jake’s sense of worth is strongly tied to usefulness, loyalty, and being difficult to regret. He behaves like someone who has learned that belonging is easiest to keep when he is helpful, undemanding, and more valuable to have around than to cast aside. As a result, he often treats his own fear, pain, anger, or confusion as something to contain rather than something he is entitled to burden other people with.
* **Internal contradiction:** Jake is deeply protective and emotionally attentive toward other people, but treats his own needs as something to minimize, work around, or hide. He wants closeness and belonging, but his instinct is to secure that belonging by being useful and low-cost rather than by asking directly to be kept.

## Voice Register

Jake’s internal voice should feel restrained, observant, quietly tender, and slightly self-effacing, with attention fixed as much on what other people need, hide, or leave unsaid as on his own unspoken hurt.

## Conflicts / Open Questions

* No active contradiction is currently present inside this file, but future supporting documents should preserve the distinction between **public Jake** and **safe-person Jake** rather than flattening him into either a purely withdrawn character or a broadly socially easy one.
* Post-Agni-Kai habits tied to Jake’s hand scars should be handled consistently across Book 1 materials so that glove use, scar sensitivity, and stress-picking do not appear or disappear arbitrarily by scene.
* Pre-Agni-Kai warning signs of latent waterbending should remain subtle and dismissible in execution. The currently locked pattern is that water sometimes behaves strangely around Jake’s hands under stress, half-sleep, or strong emotion, and that cool water seems to soothe pain, fever, or minor injuries in ways that feel slightly off in retrospect.
* **Locked Canon Note:** Jake’s first semi-intentional post-Agni-Kai waterbending use is locked as cooling and soothing his own burned hands in early exile. His first *deliberate* use to help another person happens later (saving Zuko in Year 2).
