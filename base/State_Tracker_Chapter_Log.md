# State_Tracker_Chapter_Log.md

## Purpose and Update Rules (split, 2026-10-18)

This is the **append-and-prune history layer** of the continuity system, split from `State_Tracker.md` per `workflow/Base_Files_Audit.md` finding B and the revision plan §6.2. `State_Tracker.md` holds only **current state**; per-chapter history belongs here, one file down.

- **Append** after each chapter's Gate 3: per-relationship history lines, injury/item changes, and thread provenance for the new chapter.
- **Prune** older entries only when the current-state sections above them would contradict (e.g., a resolved minor injury — move it here as history rather than delete the fact).
- Never rewrite locked canon (`Canon.md`, `Story_Bible.md`, `Relationships.md` remain authoritative). This file records only changes supported by completed chapters.
- Cross-file meaning: "what is true now" → `State_Tracker.md`; "how it got that way, chapter by chapter" → this file.

## Relationship History

### Jake ↔ Zuko — chapter log

Baseline: three-year exile survival bond per `Relationships.md`; each other's primary person in practice though neither names it cleanly.

- **Chapter 03** — Zuko kept Jake close as attendant rather than leaving him aboard, defended him when Zhao used him as insult-prop (triggering the Agni Kai challenge), pressed his shoulder in passing and ordered Iroh to stay close to him; Jake prepped Zuko for the duel and registered the ground-strike mercy as proof Zuko is not Ozai, deepening the bond from trauma-bond toward genuine respect.
- **Chapter 04** — sparred with real fire against defensive water in unspoken coordination, fought back-to-back at Kyoshi with Jake shielding Zuko from warriors and the Unagi wave; Zuko gruffly stopped Jake's trembling overexertion in the cabin.
- **Chapter 05** — Jake buffered Jee against Zuko twice (translating full-speed into half-speed through narrows; physically gripping burning wrists to break the cliff blast until fire died); Zuko listened only to Jake, withdrew without apology, then softened over tea and conceded engines night.
- **Chapter 06** — the gap widened: Zuko demanded the impossible turn, raged at the dropped sail rather than at near-death, returned wordless to the bridge, walked past Jake without looking with no word to anyone; Jake absorbed his own anger to stop Jee drawing, hauled the line to force the crew back to work, pledged "I'll handle the prince," and now dreads the necklace-locked pursuit with failing hands.
- **Chapter 07** — Zuko chose Iroh's tracks over the visible Appa lead (ribbon in hand) while Jake waited silent without pushing; Jake followed instantly at a run, shielded Zuko's back in the hollow without being asked, then walked level shoulder-to-shoulder with matched boot rhythm back to the ship; dread replaced by quiet resolve.
- **Chapter 08** — Jake stood at Zuko's shoulder on the bridge without a word as the blockade fired, hauled sandbags and dragged Kaito while refusing the sea under Fire Nation eyes, knelt to the davit ropes with wrecked hands as Zuko paid out the line without telling him to stay, rowed in wordless rhythm to Zuko's half-beat calls, caught Zuko at the gallery stair and pointed the counted turns out, swung them both across the magma fissure on a temple hoist rope cut free with the working knife (weight carried on his crossed forearms, Zuko holding his waist), and rowed clear beside him; Zuko saw the blood on the rope, lifted his hands half-way, said nothing, and rowed the second oar.
- **Chapter 09** — seamless unit: Zuko stepped half-pace right to shield Jake at the pirate table without knowing it; Jake took the rear flank and high ground, called "left," stepped into the blind-side sword arc with river water and held back-to-back without words; Zuko never saw the reveal or the eye-lock and passed Jake without a glance at the falls; Jake fell in half-step behind the left shoulder though it felt moved.
- **Chapter 10** — Zuko found Jake picking/bleeding in the alcove, cold-cloth cleaned and rebound the left palm with the second wrap and ordered him to bridge; vetoed the dry-dock with a table-slam before Jake could speak and covered it with the invented inland rumor; let his gaze touch Jake's bound hand then away, which Jake read as done-for-him at Jee's price; shielded Jake 5+ times in the woods, walked the camp perimeter before stopping, crossed the fire to hold Jake's wrist to stop picking, said "We're not going to any ports. Not until I say so," and kept watch while Jake slept.

**Secrets ledger (Jake ↔ Zuko):** none between them about Jake's identity — Zuko knows Jake is Southern Water Tribe-born and a waterbender; Jake habitually withholds his own fear, pain, and exhaustion. Chapter 06 — Jake privately recognized the torn necklace from the village-shore/ice girl and told no one why he remembered it; Chapter 07 — continued to tell no one; Chapter 08 — still untold; Chapter 09 — Zuko used the necklace openly as leverage, Jake's earlier private recognition remains untold; Chapter 10 — no new identity secrets; Zuko never names waterbending/Katara/Zhao-leak aloud, Jake never states thanks or argument, protection stays logistical and unspoken.

Baseline high trust; Iroh a safe elder and guardian, not a replacement for Haru or Emi.

- **Chapter 07** — Jake checked Iroh post-rescue in the quiet; Iroh held Jake's wrist, looked at the blood-soaked gloves, answered with plain tired gratitude and grief without scolding; Jake met it with a nod and looked away first.
