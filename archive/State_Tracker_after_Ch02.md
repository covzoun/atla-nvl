# State_Tracker.md

## Purpose and Update Rules

This is the project's **single living continuity document**. It tracks both **story state** (injuries, inventory, location, objectives, timeline, unresolved threads) and **relationship state** (trust, tension, progression, active secrets). There is no separate relationship tracker.

- Updated by the Phase 3 Continuity Clerk after each completed chapter, then verified by the human at Gate 3.
- It must never rewrite locked canon or chronology (`Canon.md`, `Story_Bible.md`, `Relationships.md` remain authoritative). It records only changes explicitly supported by the completed chapter.
- **STATE sections** (Current Story Position, Jake's Current State, Zuko's Current State, Relationship States) are **replaced** to reflect the new current state. No history is appended here.
- **LOG sections** (Unresolved Threads, Character Introductions Log, Recent Events Summary) are **appended and pruned**: threads are added when opened and marked `Resolved` (not deleted) when closed; the Recent Events Summary holds only the last 1-2 chapters, dropping older entries as new ones are added.
- Unchanged sections are preserved verbatim.

## Recent Events Summary

Chapter 01: After three years of exile patrol in southern waters, the ship sights a blue-white light and Zuko orders pursuit toward it. Two nights later Zuko loses temper in drill and burns a soldier; Jake openly douses the flames with barrel water crew-side. Zuko rejects Iroh's sleep warning and armors with Jake's aid. A Fire Navy flare leads Zuko's spyglass to two running figures and their village; the ship closes while Jake holds quiet dread.

Chapter 02: The ship breaches the ice wall and Zuko raids the village, beating back club and boomerang attacks. A penguin-riding arrow-marked boy reveals himself as the Avatar and surrenders, and is taken aboard with his staff secured in Zuko's quarters. The boy escapes via airbending, a mattress-slam, and a glider leap; Zuko drags both to the lower deck before a flying creature arrives with two riders. Zuko knocks the boy into the sea, triggering a white-eyed waterspout and deck-clearing wave that Jake subtly redirects to save crew; the unnamed girl freezes guards, a combined fire blast is deflected into the cliff, and the resulting avalanche buries the bow. The ship lists trapped in ice while Jake triages crew and Zuko orders dig-out and renewed pursuit.
