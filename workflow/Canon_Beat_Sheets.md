# Canon Beat Sheets — Book 1, Chapters 1–10

Date: 2026-10-18
Purpose: for each Book 1 chapter, the five to eight **canon events that must survive** an adaptation, in order and unaltered. Revisions are checked against these sheets, **not against memory** (revision plan §6.2). A chapter may add, compress, or re-frame around these beats; it may not delete, reorder, or alter them.

## How to use

- Tags: **[on-page]** = the beat happens inside the chapter and is dramatized. **[off-screen]** = the beat happens in the world while the chapter stays with the ship-side, and the prose must not contradict or "learn" it (no chapter actor may know it unless canonically informed later).
- For **Original Content chapters** the sheet anchors to the Macro Map B-plot instead of a synopsis; the episode playing off-screen still happens exactly as canon and must remain untouched.
- AU overlays are stated as constraints under each sheet; gold-standard sources for overlays are `Book_1_Macro_Map.md` locks and `State_Tracker.md`.

## Standing cross-checks for every revised chapter

1. Every required beat **present, in order, unaltered** (structural edits may change *packaging*, never the event).
2. No chapter actor learns an [off-screen] beat's content unless a later canon moment legitimately informs them.
3. Overlays must respect Macro Map locks and knowledge limits (`State_Tracker.md` records what actually got known when).
4. The `Ep` synopses in `source/Season 01/` are the authority for [off-screen] and [on-page] canon content; the sheet marks where each sits.5. Chapters 11–20 get their sheets as they are planned (add below this line, same format).
